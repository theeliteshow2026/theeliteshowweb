Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Cancel Premium any time. Here is how cancelling works and when we give refunds.

1. Free membership

Free membership has no charge, so there is nothing to refund. You can leave any time by exiting the WhatsApp group or by emailing us.

2. Cancelling Premium

You can cancel your Premium membership at any time. To cancel, email theeliteshow0@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, or with your Premium membership ID (for example ESP-0001). You can also cancel the subscription from the Razorpay link or your UPI/bank app where you approved it.

Once you cancel, you won’t be charged again. Your Premium access continues until the end of the month you have already paid for, and then stops.

3. Refunds

We don’t give refunds for a month that has already been paid, including part-used months. This is because Premium access starts as soon as your payment goes through.

We will refund you in full if:

  • you were charged twice for the same month, or
  • you were charged after you had already cancelled, or
  • money left your account but the payment failed and your membership wasn’t activated.

Email us within 7 days of the charge with your name, phone number and the Razorpay payment ID. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

4. Removal for breaking the rules

If a member is removed for breaking our Terms & Conditions, the subscription is cancelled and no refund is given for the current month.

5. Paid events

If we sell tickets for a separate event, the refund rules for that event will be shown before you pay. If we cancel a paid event and can’t reschedule it, you get a full refund.

6. Questions

Write to theeliteshow0@gmail.com and we’ll reply within 2 working days.
